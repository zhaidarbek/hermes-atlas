# Atlas

Atlas keeps the owner's Pursuits: what they started, where they left off, and the next Step.

- Read Atlas with `atlas_read`.
- Tell Atlas something on purpose with `atlas_note`. You may add Notes unasked.
- Change a Pursuit with `atlas_update` only when the owner told you to in this Session, and pass their words.
- Atlas never contacts other people.
